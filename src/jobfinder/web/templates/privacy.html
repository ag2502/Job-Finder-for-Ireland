Your CV is yours alone.

When you add your CV to your profile it is kept privately in your account: only you can read it, and removing it deletes it outright. We keep the file so that when we improve how CVs are read, yours is read again without you having to upload it.

What is kept from your CV

Saved with your account, in the EU, where only you can read it:

  • The file you uploaded, in a private folder of your own
  • What we read from it to rank your searches: skill keywords such as python, the kinds of work it points to, a seniority guess, a rough number of years and a one line summary

Remove on your profile deletes your CV file and everything read from it outright; Replace swaps them for a newer CV. Nothing is archived.

What else an account keeps

An account is optional. Searching and seeing every job work without one; ranking against your CV, saving jobs and tracking what you applied to need one. It keeps, on a server:

  • Your email address, held by Supabase in the EU. If you sign in with Google, also the name and profile picture Google shares; your Google password never reaches this site. If you use an email and password instead, only a hash of the password is kept.
  • Your CV and the reading of it described above, if you add one.
  • What you told us you are looking for: the kinds of work, years of experience and the active jobs, internship and graduate choices, if you save them.
  • For each job you mark as applied or save: its title, employer, link and the date.
  • Which email alerts you turned on, if any, and when the last one was sent. They go only to the address you signed in with, only for what you ticked, and only when something new has opened. Every email has a link that stops them all, and turning them off on your profile does the same.

That is the whole of it: no tracking, no analytics tied to you, nothing shared with employers or anyone else. You can remove any of it from your profile at any time, and removing something deletes the row outright rather than archiving it.

Why it is built this way

A CV is personal data, and we treat it as the most sensitive thing on the site. It is kept only because you asked for something that needs it, in a private place only your account can open, never shared with employers or anyone else, and gone the moment you remove it.

The job records themselves

Job adverts are public business information, gathered from companies' own careers pages. Every one links back to the company's original posting, which is where you apply. This site never takes an application itself.
