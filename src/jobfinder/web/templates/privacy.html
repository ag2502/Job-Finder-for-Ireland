What happens to your CV

Your CV is never stored. It is read in memory, and the file is discarded before the page loads. Nothing is written to disk or to a database.

What is kept

Only what matching needs, held in a signed cookie in your own browser:

  • Skill keywords found in your CV (e.g. python, kubernetes)
  • A seniority guess and rough years of experience
  • The fields you chose
  • A short excerpt of CV text used for keyword scoring

Clearing your session with Start over, or clearing your browser cookies, removes all of it. There is no account and no server-side copy to request or delete.

Why it is built this way

A CV is personal data. Under GDPR, storing one makes the operator a data controller with retention, access and erasure duties, and it becomes the most sensitive thing in the system — the part worth attacking. Extracting the few hundred bytes that matching actually uses and discarding the document removes that risk entirely, and costs you nothing in results quality.

Job listings

Job postings are public business information collected from employers' own careers systems. Each listing links back to the employer's original posting, which is where you apply.
